# 🍕 College Canteen Improvement Challenge — Step-by-Step Prompt

**Objective:** You are tasked with improving a college canteen for one week with a budget of **₹10,000**. Your goal is to create a comprehensive plan that maximizes profit, satisfies student demand, and uses the budget wisely.

---

### Step 1: Understand the Constraints
- Total budget: ₹10,000 (for the entire week)
- Timeframe: 7 days (one week)
- Context: College canteen serving students
- You must account for ingredient costs, preparation, and any overheads

### Step 2: Research and Decide on the Menu
- Choose **5–8 food items** to offer for the week
- Consider:
  - **Popular items** among college students (e.g., pizza slices, samosas, noodles, sandwiches, chai, burgers)
  - **Cost per unit** for each item
  - **Prep time** — can it be made quickly during peak hours?
  - **Shelf life** — will the food stay fresh for the week?
- Balance between **snacks, meals, and beverages**

### Step 3: Calculate Pricing and Profit Margins
- For each item, determine:
  - **Cost price** (ingredients + labor per unit)
  - **Selling price** (must be affordable for students)
  - **Profit margin** per unit
- Ensure the total cost of ingredients and supplies does **not exceed ₹10,000**
- Target at least **40–60% profit margin** on each item

### Step 4: Plan Quantities and Daily Breakdown
- Estimate **daily demand** for each item
- Create a **day-by-day breakdown** (Monday–Sunday):
  - How many units of each item to prepare each day
  - Adjust quantities based on **peak days** (e.g., weekends) vs. **slow days** (e.g., exam weeks)
- Factor in **waste reduction** — don't over-prepare

### Step 5: Demand Management Strategy
- Describe how you will **manage demand**:
  - Pre-order system or queue management?
  - Peak-hour strategies (e.g., special combos during lunch break)
  - How to handle **unexpected surges** or shortages
  - Any **loyalty/discount system** to attract repeat customers?
- Include a plan for **inventory tracking** day-to-day

### Step 6: Budget Allocation Summary
- Provide a **clear budget table** showing:
  | Category | Item | Cost |
  |---|---|---|
  | Ingredients | ... | ₹X |
  | Packaging | ... | ₹X |
  | Miscellaneous | ... | ₹X |
  | **Total** | | **₹10,000** |
- Ensure the budget is fully accounted for

### Step 7: Expected Outcome
- Estimate the **total revenue** for the week
- Estimate the **total profit**
- Justify why your plan is **optimal** for a college canteen setting

---

**Final Output Format:** Your response should be structured as a **detailed report** with tables, numbers, and a clear action plan — ready to be handed to the canteen manager for execution.
